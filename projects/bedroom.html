The scheme

Same in both rooms (see the 3D model, Reno › Bedroom, and BEDROOM-LIGHTING.md for the reasoning).

  • Pendant on the existing ceiling rose: Lucci August oval, opal glass, 400 × 240, bottom at ~2.35 m, on a dimmer.
  • LED strip on top of the robe: in an angled aluminium channel at the back of the robe top, tilted up into the room, washing the ceiling and cornice. Front room: runs over the robe and the open shelving.
  • LED strip under the robe: same tape, in a slim channel tucked under the door bottoms in the plinth recess, a low glow on the floor.
  • Both strips on one 24 V driver per room, on their own dimmer (the second light button in the app).
  • Robes painted white, a 20-30 mm lip along the front of the robe top, silver round knobs.
  • 2700 K everywhere, CRI 90+ (95+ for the tape), one tape batch for all strips.
  • Reading: bedside lamps. Optional: light inside the robes that comes on with the doors. Cream carpet.
