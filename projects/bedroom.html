Stage 1: front bedroom, bedroom 2, hall

13 Fern Ave. What to buy, what to measure, what to tell the sparky. Tap Copy everything to paste the lot into Claude.

The scheme in one breath

  • Warm everywhere: 2700 K, CRI 90+ (95+ for LED tape), no downlights in the bedrooms or hall.
  • Each bedroom: one opal pendant on the ceiling rose + an LED strip hidden on top of the robe + a faint strip under the robe doors at the floor. Two dimmers per room.
  • Hall: three matching opal pendants down the centre, bottoms at 2.1 m, switched from both ends.
  • White robes with silver knobs, cream carpet, sheers in the bay (front room), sheers + white roller blind (bedroom 2).

Buy (China sourcing list)

Switches, sockets, drivers and anything hard-wired must carry the AU RCM mark or the electrician can't legally fit it. Pendants and lamps are the easy wins to import; keep the wiring gear AU-compliant.

Pendants

  • 2 × bedroom pendant: frosted opal glass oval, ~400 wide × 240 high, white canopy, cord for 3.15 m ceiling, bottom of shade at 2.35 m. E27. (Model: Lucci August oval.)
  • 3 × hall pendant: frosted opal glass "pebble", ~310 wide × 280 high, small white cap, cord for 3.15 m ceiling, bottom at 2.1 m. E27.
  • 5 × E27 LED globe: frosted, 2700 K, CRI 90+, dimmable (trailing-edge). Bedrooms 1200–1500 lm (opal glass eats ~30%). Hall ~800 lm.
  • 2 × small plain plaster ceiling rose, ~300 dia, for the two new hall pendants (the entry rose is original, keep).

LED strips (both bedrooms)

  • Tape: 24 V, 2700 K, CRI 95+, SDCM ≤ 3, ≥ 120 LEDs/m or COB, 700–1000 lm/m class (run dimmed). Need ~3.1 m front robe + shelving, ~3.3 m bedroom 2 robe, plus the same again for the floor strips: buy 2 × 5 m reels + 1 × 5 m spare.
  • Channel, top of robe: 45° angled aluminium channel with opal diffuser, 2 × ~3.3 m (tape faces up and back at the wall). The robe stops ~0.55 m below the ceiling so the glow washes the ceiling and cornice.
  • Channel, floor: slim recessed/flat aluminium channel with opal diffuser, 2 × ~3.3 m, tucked in the plinth recess under the doors. Run at ~5% as a night light.
  • Drivers: 2 × 24 V dimmable constant-voltage driver, 60 W, trailing-edge (Triac) dimmable, RCM, one per room, hidden in the robe top. Alternative: Zigbee/Tuya 24 V dimmer-drivers and smart wall dimmers, which sidesteps dimmer-compatibility trouble.
  • End caps, mounting clips, 2-core 24 V cable, solder/clip connectors.

Switches (AU standard, white, RCM)

  • Front bedroom, inside north of the door: 1 rotary dimmer (pendant) + 1 rocker (robe strips). The door is rehung to open against the shelving.
  • Bedroom 2, inside north of the door: 1 dimmer (pendant) + 1 rocker (robe strips). Door rehung likewise.
  • Entry, west wall inside the front door (existing switch position, lowered to 1.05 m): 1 dimmer, 2-way (hall pendants) + 1 rocker (porch).
  • Kitchen end of the hall: 1 × 2-way rocker for the hall pendants (the rest of the kitchen plate comes in stage 2).
  • All plates centre 1.05 m, latch side, ~100 mm off the architrave. Dimmers trailing-edge, LED-rated. Clipsal Iconic or equal; Chinese "AU standard" plates exist but check the RCM mark.

Power points (double GPO, white, RCM; USB-C on the bedside ones)

  • Front bedroom: 2 × bedside (one each side of the bed head), 1 × desk/dresser wall, 1 × inside the robe top for the driver (switched). 4 doubles.
  • Bedroom 2: 2 × bedside, 1 × desk wall, 1 × robe top for the driver. 4 doubles.
  • Hall: 1 × double low on the party wall for the vacuum.
  • Split systems (installer supplies the isolators): 3.5 kW front bedroom, 2.5 kW bedroom 2, both on the west wall, tops at 2.65 m.

Other

  • Robe knobs: small round silver, ~30 mm, 10 for the front room (4 doors + drawers), 8 for bedroom 2.
  • Bedroom 2 roller blind: white blockout, ~1.47 m wide, face-fit cassette above the architrave. Sheer curtain track, ceiling-fix, ~1.7 m.
  • Front bay: sheer track ~2.8 m ceiling-fix across the bay, sheers gathered to each side.

Measure on site before ordering

Model numbers are prefilled; overwrite with the tape. These go into the copied text.

Notes

Tell the electrician

  • Pendants hung from a joist through the rose, never from the plaster. New roses on the hall centreline at the two marked points.
  • Two circuits per bedroom: ceiling (dimmer) and strips (rocker, dimmed at the driver or a second dimmer).
  • Hall pendants 2-way: entry and kitchen end, on one trailing-edge dimmer.
  • Drivers in the robe tops on a switched GPO; 24 V runs inside the robe to the floor channel.
  • Remove the ducted-heating floor grille and controller. Patch the old high switch boxes.
  • All lamps and strips 2700 K; nothing cooler anywhere in the house.
