How these were shot

Following common real-estate photography practice: camera at chest height (1.3 m) and kept level, so walls and door frames stay vertical, with the frame shifted up (as a tilt-shift lens does) to take in the ceilings and pendants. A 16-24 mm full-frame-equivalent lens for the rooms and a longer one for the two detail shots (the painting and the Amalfi cluster), no fisheye. Rooms shot from a corner to show two or three walls, the hall straight down its length. 4:3 landscape at 2400 x 1800, the realestate.com.au ratio. Dusk with all the lights on, so the windows read deep blue rather than black.
